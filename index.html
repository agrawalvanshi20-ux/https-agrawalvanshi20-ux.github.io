Hello

I keep a neuroscience lab running, and I came to it through immunology.

I am a lab and facilities technician in the Nuffield Department of Clinical Neurosciences at Oxford. Before this I spent two years as an assistant lab technician in the School of Food Science and Nutrition at the University of Leeds, where I also did my MSc in Infection, Immunology and Human Disease. My first degree was in biotechnology, in India, and I finished it with first class honours.

The brain is the thing I have kept circling back to since I started reading immunology properly, so a neurosciences department is exactly where I wanted to end up.

Food is the other thread, and I have been lucky that it turned into work rather than staying a thing I did at home. Two years in food science and nutrition gave me a whole second set of questions about what we eat and what it does to us, and I have no intention of putting that one down.

Day to day, my job is the unglamorous half of research: the equipment working, the stocks in, the protocols followed, the samples where they should be. Good science rests on it.

The route here

  1. 2026 to now Lab and facilities technician Nuffield Department of Clinical Neurosciences, University of Oxford
  2. 2 years Assistant lab technician School of Food Science and Nutrition, University of Leeds
  3. MSc Infection, Immunology and Human Disease University of Leeds. Lab-based research project in virology
  4. BSc Biotechnology, first class India
